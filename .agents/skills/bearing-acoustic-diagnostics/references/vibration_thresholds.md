# ISO-10816-3 CNC Spindle Vibration & Acoustic Emission Thresholds

## 1. ISO-10816-3 Velocity Severity Zones (10 Hz – 1000 Hz, Rigid Mount)
- **Zone A (Good / New Commissioning):** `< 1.4 mm/s RMS`
- **Zone B (Acceptable Continuous Operation):** `1.4 – 2.8 mm/s RMS`
- **Zone C (Unsatisfactory / Schedule Intervention):** `2.8 – 4.5 mm/s RMS`
- **Zone D (Critical Damage Risk / Immediate Hold):** `> 4.5 mm/s RMS`

## 2. High-Frequency Acoustic Emission & Bearing Characteristic Frequencies
- **Angular Contact Spindle Bearing (`7014C-T-P4S`, 18 Balls, 3600–6000 RPM):**
  - **BPFO (Outer Race Fault):** `7.85 x Shaft Frequency` + high-frequency carrier resonance at `4.21 kHz`.
  - **Spindle Bearing Housing Temperature Limit:** Nominal `38 °C – 48 °C`; Alarm `> 55 °C`; Trip `> 65 °C`.
  - **Drawbar Clamping Force & Locknut Torque:** `145 Nm` calibrated locknut torque; `< 3 micron` dynamic runout TIR.
